This policy governs material use of artificial intelligence, machine learning,
automated moderation, assistants, generated content, recommendations, and
similar systems within SpaceMountain.Live (**SPMT**). It supplements the
applicable Partner, Crew, or Developer Community Terms.

## 1. Human responsibility and oversight

AI systems may assist with drafting, classification, triage, recommendations,
support, moderation, safety review, coding, or content generation. They do not
replace ownership authority, legal judgment, or the fair-process requirements
in the applicable Terms.

A material adverse decision affecting role status, access, discipline,
eligibility, payment, certification, listing, or a rights complaint must not be
based solely on unreviewed AI output. An authorized human must review the
relevant facts, consider reasonably available context, and remain accountable
for the decision.

## 2. Transparency

SPMT should provide reasonable notice when a person is interacting primarily
with an AI assistant or when AI materially influences a moderation,
recommendation, or eligibility process. The level of notice may vary by risk,
context, security, and technical practicality.

Developers must identify material AI features, model providers, external data
flows, and automated decision functions in the Manifest, privacy notice, or
review materials when reasonably requested.

## 3. Generated content and user responsibility

A person who submits, publishes, or deploys AI-generated or AI-assisted content
remains responsible for that content. AI use does not excuse infringement,
harassment, deception, privacy violations, unsafe code, unlawful conduct, or
breach of provider terms.

AI-generated content must not be presented as a verified fact, official SPMT
statement, human-authored testimony, or professional advice when that would be
misleading. Material synthetic media should be labeled when a reasonable person
could otherwise mistake it for an authentic record or statement.

## 4. Data, privacy, and training

SPMT and Developers must minimize personal and confidential data sent to AI
providers, use approved access controls, and avoid placing credentials,
recovery codes, payment data, private investigation records, or unnecessary
sensitive information into prompts or training pipelines.

Neither SPMT nor a Developer may use another party's private data to train an
unrelated model, sell profiles, or create an undisclosed identity graph without
a lawful basis, clear notice, and any required consent or agreement.

## 5. Safety and prohibited uses

AI systems must not be used to impersonate ownership or another person,
fabricate evidence, evade moderation or security controls, conduct unlawful
surveillance, make prohibited eligibility inferences, manipulate rewards or
votes, generate malicious code for unauthorized access, or automate harassment,
fraud, exploitation, or other unlawful conduct.

SPMT may restrict an AI feature that creates material safety, privacy,
security, rights, provider, or legal risk.

## 6. Accuracy, testing, and appeal

AI outputs must be treated as potentially incomplete or incorrect. Material AI
features should be tested for reliability, security, privacy, foreseeable bias,
failure modes, and appropriate fallback behavior before production use.

Where an AI-assisted process materially affects a person, the person should
have access to the existing ticket, statement, review, correction, or appeal
process available under the applicable Terms. SPMT may preserve relevant model,
prompt, version, and decision metadata when lawful and proportionate.

## 7. Changes

AI technology and regulation change quickly. Material changes to data use,
automated decision authority, participant duties, or liability require a
versioned update and any affirmative reacceptance required by the applicable
Community Terms.
