This policy provides the dedicated route for copyright, Digital Millennium
Copyright Act (DMCA), trademark, and related rights reports concerning content,
applications, listings, branding, or services within SpaceMountain.Live
(**SPMT**). It supplements the applicable Partner, Crew, or Developer Community
Terms.

## 1. Where to send a report

Ownership should publish a current legal-reporting contact and secure intake
route as soon as reasonably practicable. Until that route is available, a
reporter may send a private message through an official SPMT or Discord account
to the Owner, Co-Owner, or designated written proxy and request a
legal-reporting ticket.

The published route should issue a reference ID and preserve the report in a
durable, access-controlled record. Public posts, ordinary moderation channels,
and unrelated support tickets are not preferred legal-notice routes.

## 2. Copyright and DMCA notices

A copyright notice should include:

- the reporter's full legal name and reliable contact information;
- identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed;
- identification and location of the allegedly infringing material with enough
  detail for SPMT to find it;
- a statement of good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by
  the rights holder, its agent, or law;
- a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the notice is accurate and that
  the reporter is authorized to act for the rights holder; and
- a physical or electronic signature.

SPMT may request missing information before acting. Where the DMCA or another
law applies, SPMT may remove or disable access to material, notify the affected
user, preserve relevant records, process a valid counter-notice, and restore
material when legally appropriate.

A counter-notice should identify the removed material and its prior location,
include the required good-faith and jurisdiction statements, provide the
submitter's contact information, and include a physical or electronic
signature. SPMT may forward notices and counter-notices to the affected parties
as permitted or required by law.

## 3. Trademark and related rights reports

A trademark or related rights report should identify the mark or right, the
registration or other basis for the claim when available, the reporter's
authority, the disputed use and location, the likelihood of confusion or other
claimed harm, and reliable contact information.

SPMT may request supporting documents, proof of ownership or authority, and a
good-faith declaration. Trademark reports are evaluated under applicable law
and platform context; ownership of a mark does not automatically prohibit every
reference, commentary, comparison, or nominative use.

## 4. Review and interim action

SPMT may temporarily hide, restrict, preserve, delist, or disable access to
disputed material while reviewing a report. SPMT may consider the notice,
counter-notice, licenses, ownership records, fair-use or other defenses,
platform context, prior reports, user safety, and applicable law.

Rights reports are separate from conduct or app-review matters, although the
same facts may support both processes. A legal report does not guarantee removal
or restoration, and SPMT does not adjudicate ownership disputes beyond the
limited decisions needed to operate its services.

## 5. Repeat and abusive reports

SPMT may restrict or terminate accounts or projects associated with repeated
infringement when appropriate under applicable law and the relevant Community
Terms. Knowingly false, fraudulent, abusive, or retaliatory reports or
counter-notices may lead to suspension, termination, legal referral, or other
action.

## 6. Privacy, records, and disclosure

SPMT will limit access to rights reports to people who need the information for
review, response, legal compliance, or system protection. Reports may be shared
with the affected user, service providers, legal advisers, authorities, or
other parties when reasonably necessary or legally required.

SPMT may retain notices, counter-notices, evidence, decisions, and related
communications for the period reasonably needed for legal compliance,
repeat-infringer review, dispute handling, and record integrity.

## 7. Changes

Material changes affecting participant rights, duties, data use, liability, or
dispute handling require a versioned update and any reacceptance required by the
applicable Community Terms and Electronic Acceptance Schedule.

## 8. No legal advice

This policy is an operational reporting process and does not provide legal
advice. Reporters and affected users remain responsible for understanding their
rights and obligations and should obtain qualified legal advice when needed.
