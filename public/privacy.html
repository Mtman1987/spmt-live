SpaceMountain.Live Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective August 3, 2026 · Illinois, United States

This Privacy Policy explains how SpaceMountain.Live (“SPMT,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use SPMT, DiscordStreamHub, SpaceMountain, StreamWeaver, HearMeOut, ChatTag, Quackverse, MountainView, and other first-party ecosystem services.

1. Information we collect

We may collect account and profile information such as your SPMT username, display name, password hash, recovery information, linked Discord or Twitch identifiers, avatar, roles, and preferences. We may also collect application submissions, agreement acceptances, messages, notifications, forum content, app settings, moderation records, votes, support requests, uploaded content, and other information you choose to provide.

When you use the Services, we may collect technical and usage information such as IP address, browser or device details, session and authentication records, timestamps, app events, error logs, security events, audit history, and feature interactions. We may receive information from connected services such as Discord, Twitch, OAuth providers, hosting providers, and payment providers according to the permissions you grant.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; authenticate users; provide messages, apps, integrations, moderation, support, and community features; process role and developer applications; record legal acceptance; prevent fraud and abuse; diagnose outages; improve reliability and accessibility; comply with law and provider requirements; and communicate service, safety, and policy updates.

3. AI processing

Some features may use AI-assisted moderation, support, recommendations, drafting, or generated content. We minimize personal and confidential data sent to AI providers and do not intentionally place credentials, recovery codes, payment data, or unnecessary sensitive records into AI prompts. Additional rules appear in our AI Policy.

4. When we share information

We may share information with service providers that host infrastructure, deliver authentication, operate messaging or media, process payments, provide security or analytics, or otherwise support the Services. We may also share information with connected applications at your direction, with authorized reviewers or owners for governance and safety, or when required to comply with law, enforce agreements, protect rights and safety, or respond to valid legal process.

We do not sell personal information. We do not permit third parties to use private SPMT data for unrelated advertising or model training without a lawful basis and appropriate notice or consent.

5. Public and community information

Information you post in public profiles, forums, Discord channels, streams, leaderboards, overlays, or community surfaces may be visible to others and may be copied or redistributed outside our control. Use care when posting personal information publicly.

6. Cookies and sessions

We use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies for authentication, security, preferences, session continuity, and app functionality. Disabling them may prevent parts of the Services from working. We do not use them to promise cross-site advertising tracking.

7. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain security and audit records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and enforce agreements. Account and ordinary content may be deleted or de-identified when no longer needed. Acceptance records, major moderation findings, security records, and ownership decisions may be retained longer where required for integrity, accountability, or law.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, including access controls, hashed credentials, authenticated sessions, logging, backups, and least-privilege practices. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, loss, or misuse will never occur.

9. Your choices and requests

You may update certain account information through the Services and may request access, correction, export, or deletion of eligible personal information through the authenticated support or private ticket channel. Some information may be retained where necessary for security, legal obligations, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, or immutable acceptance and audit records. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

10. Children and eligibility

The Services are not directed to children below the minimum age permitted by applicable law or connected platforms. If we learn that ineligible information was collected, we may restrict the account and delete eligible data. Misrepresenting age or eligibility may result in loss of participation.

11. International processing

Service providers may process information in locations outside your state or country. Where required, we use appropriate legal and contractual measures for those transfers.

12. Changes

We may update this Policy as the Services, providers, or law change. Material changes will be published with a new effective date and, when appropriate, presented for renewed acceptance.

13. Contact

Privacy questions and requests may be submitted through the authenticated SPMT support or private ticket channel made available in the Services.
