Connect your PS5 gameplay

Save your overlays to Public Output in the overlay builder first. This adds PS5 picture and sound to that same saved scene.

Checking your account and receiver…

1. Connect your console’s network

Open this page while connected to the same home network as your PS5. On a phone, use home Wi-Fi.

If your browser uses IPv6 or is on another network, enter the PS5 home network’s public IPv4.

2. Set PS5 DNS and broadcast

On PS5, open Settings → Network → Settings → Set Up Internet Connection. Select your network, open Advanced Settings, and set DNS Settings → Manual.

Primary DNS: Waiting for receiver…
Secondary DNS: 0.0.0.0

Save, then use PS5’s Create → Broadcast → Twitch with thecaptaindash’s Twitch account. The receiver sends gameplay to your scene.

After testing, restore DNS to Automatic if you want to broadcast directly from the console again. If the network changes, reconnect it using step 1.

3. Check picture and sound

Waiting for gameplay.

The preview uses bandwidth and plays sound locally. Close this page after checking it.

4. Use your single scene URL in Restream

Open my Restream hostCheck my full scene

In your hosted Restream browser, sign in, connect your Twitch destination, and open Studio. Choose Widgets → Add, paste this URL, and maximize the widget in your landscape scene. Check gameplay, overlays, and audio, then click Go Live.

You can close the controller after going live. Your dedicated Studio host stays open in the background. End the broadcast in Restream when you are finished; Stop host closes the hosted browser.
