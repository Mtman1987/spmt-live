Switch hosts between broadcasts. End the broadcast and stop the old host first. Closing a cloud controller does not stop its host.

2. Create or sign in to your free Restream account

Open Restream in the host you chose. Create your free account there, complete any verification, then connect your Twitch channel. Choose the same Twitch account you use with SPMT.

With Companion, use Use my Companion above. With a cloud host, use its private controller. Your Restream login must be completed in that host, so signing in in a different browser won't connect it.

These checks record your progress; they do not verify Restream authorization.

3. Add your SPMT overlay

Enter Studio. Choose Widgets → Add, name it SpaceMountain, and paste your link below. Add the widget to your landscape scene. Keep your SPMT layout together in this one widget for the free plan.

4. Connect gameplay and test

Broadcast your PS5 to YouTube, then add its live link below. Your saved SPMT scene and gameplay use the same free Restream widget. This route adds broadcast delay and needs a picture/audio check in Restream before going live.

  1. On PS5: Settings → Users and Accounts → Linked Services → YouTube. Link his own YouTube account and enable live streaming. First activation can take up to 24 hours.
  2. Open a game. Press Create → Broadcast → YouTube and start a test broadcast. Choose Unlisted if available; Private broadcasts cannot play in the cloud widget.
  3. On a phone, open YouTube Studio and copy the current broadcast's share link. Use a broadcast link, not a channel link. Allow embedding in YouTube Studio.
  4. Save the link below. This adds PS5 Gameplay to your public Overlay Bay scene, beneath your existing overlays. Position it there if needed.
  5. In your cloud Restream Studio, add your SPMT link from step 3 as the single widget and maximize it. Check moving gameplay, game audio, and overlays. Keep YouTube disabled as a Restream destination to avoid feeding the output back into its input.
